How it works

Three steps between your dog and the yard.

Every dog in the yard is vetted, vaccinated, and friendly — that's the whole point. Registration takes a few minutes online and you only do it once.

1

Register online

Tell us about your pup and how they do with other dogs. Takes about five minutes — do it before you come or from the bar.

2

We check vaccines

Upload your dog's vaccination records. A human on our team reviews every one before your pup is cleared for the yard.

3

Come play

Grab a day pass or membership, unclip the leash, and order yourself something cold. We'll handle the rest.
